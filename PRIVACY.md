# Privacy note

Travel Assistant is a static website. It does not send trip details, budget entries, or checklist data to a server.

- Trip plans, budget values are not stored as a history by the app.
- Saved trips and checklist state are stored in the browser's `localStorage` on the device.
- Clearing browser site data may erase locally saved information.
- External links (for example, travel search providers) open third-party websites that have their own privacy policies and terms.
- Do not enter sensitive personal, payment, passport, or identity information into notes.

This note is informational and is not a substitute for a legal privacy policy if the project is later expanded into a service that collects personal data.
